uber-bookr

Connect your Uber account

Install the uber-bookr Chrome extension, log in to uber.com, then click Connect in the extension popup.

Setup steps:

  1. Open chrome://extensions
  2. Enable Developer mode (top right)
  3. Click "Load unpacked" and select the extension/ folder
  4. Go to uber.com and log in
  5. Click the extension icon and hit Connect
